How it works

From the container to the doorstep

Every shipment follows the same six steps, and each one leaves a record you can check.

  1. 01

    Documents in

    Our admin team receives your import documents and plans for the arrival.

  2. 02

    We collect it

    When the container or air freight lands, we bring it into the right depot.

  3. 03

    Unloaded and checked

    Cartons, loose cargo and pallets are sorted and checked against the manifest.

  4. 04

    Booked into the system

    Each shipment gets its own consignment number and tracking link.

  5. 05

    Scanned at every move

    Warehouse scans record each movement before drivers load for delivery.

  6. 06

    Signed for

    The receiver signs on delivery, and that proof is attached to your consignment.

Tracking

Know where your freight is, any time

Every consignment is tracked in Transvirtual, our transport management system, so you can check progress yourself instead of waiting on a reply.

  1. 1

    Get your consignment number

    We send it with your booking confirmation, or as a tracking link by email.

  2. 2

    Enter it here or open your link

    You’ll see each scan as your freight moves through our depots and onto the truck.

  3. 3

    See proof of delivery

    Once it’s delivered, the receiver’s signature is available on the same record.

Quality control

We check it, so it isn’t rejected

Behind every shipment is a business that has put money and effort into its product. We treat it that way.

  • The Phoenix red QC table

    Freight is inspected on our quality-control table so damaged or mislabelled cartons are caught before they ship, not after.

  • Machine wrapping

    Pallets are stretch-wrapped by machine for consistent, stable loads that survive the trip.

  • Checked against the paperwork

    Counts and labels are checked against the manifest so fewer shipments bounce back with avoidable rejections.

About us

A family business, 20 years in the making

Phoenix Global Logistics is run by a family that has spent more than two decades in freight. Before Phoenix, they ran Phoenix International Cargo for around 15 years and learned the industry the hard way: the mistakes, the lessons and what customers actually need.

We started Phoenix Global Logistics in 2021 to do it better and bigger. Today we have depots in Sydney and Melbourne, our own vehicles, a dedicated team and partners in China and around the world.
